FireExtCue Support

FireExtCue keeps household fire extinguisher checks, dates, cycles, notes, and activity on your device.

English

Track pressure gauge checks, access path, pin and tamper seal, hose and nozzle, body condition, instruction labels, monthly checks, and professional service follow-up. Set your own dates, reminder lead times, and notes. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

Apple Reminders is optional. A saved task and local notifications remain available when Apple Reminders permission is not granted.

FireExtCue is a reminder and record tool. It does not guarantee fire safety, extinguisher performance, code compliance, or inspection approval. Follow the extinguisher label, manufacturer guidance, and local requirements. For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
