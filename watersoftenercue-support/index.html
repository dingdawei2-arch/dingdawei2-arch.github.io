WaterSoftenerCue Support

WaterSoftenerCue keeps water softener maintenance tasks, dates, cycles, notes, and activity on your device.

English

Start with Salt level check, Salt refill, Salt bridge check, Brine tank clean, Regeneration observation, Hardness strip log, Resin cleaner, and Professional service. You can set your own dates, reminder lead times, and notes. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

Apple Reminders is optional. A saved task and local notifications remain available when Apple Reminders permission is not granted.

For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.

简体中文

默认包含盐位检查、补盐、盐桥检查、盐箱清理、再生观察、硬度试纸记录、树脂清洁和专业服务。你可以自行设置日期、提前提醒和备注。本地通知和小组件会显示下一项任务。

Apple 提醒事项为可选项。未授予 Apple 提醒事项权限不会影响已经保存的任务和本地通知。
