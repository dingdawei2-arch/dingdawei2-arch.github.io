ColorCatch Privacy

简体中文

ColorCatch 会请求权限以展示更相关的广告并衡量广告效果。即使你拒绝，游戏仍可继续游玩。

ColorCatch 会在设备和 App Group 中保存最高分，用于主 App 和小组件显示。永久去广告购买由 Apple 处理，ColorCatch 不保存支付信息。

English

ColorCatch asks permission to show more relevant ads and measure ad performance. The game remains playable if you decline.

ColorCatch stores the best score on device and in its App Group for the main app and widget. Permanent remove ads purchases are processed by Apple, and ColorCatch does not store payment information.
