DishwasherCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-14

English

DishwasherCue stores task names, categories, locations, dates, cycles, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets.

Notification permission is optional and is used for local task reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated tasks.

DishwasherCue does not collect data or include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may recommend another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap. DishwasherCue does not create accounts or use cloud sync.

简体中文

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地任务提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，只在你选择同步带日期任务后使用。

DishwasherCue 不收集数据，也不包含第三方广告或追踪 SDK。它可能推荐同一开发者的另一款 App；只有你主动点击后才会打开 App Store。 DishwasherCue 不创建账号，也不使用云同步。
