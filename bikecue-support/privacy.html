BikeCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-13

English

BikeCue stores task names, categories, locations, dates, cycles, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets.

Notification permission is optional and is used for local task reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated tasks.

Free users may see clearly labeled Google AdMob native ads. Plus subscribers stay ad-free. Depending on your ATT choice and device settings, Google may process ad identifiers, device information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. BikeCue does not create accounts or use cloud sync.

简体中文

BikeCue 会将任务名称、类别、位置、日期、周期、提前提醒、备注、完成次数、漏做次数和最近活动保存在你的设备以及小组件使用的 App Group 容器中。

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地任务提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，只在你选择同步带日期任务后使用。

免费用户可能看到明确标注的 Google AdMob 原生广告。Plus 订阅用户无广告。根据你的 ATT 选择和设备设置，Google 可能处理广告标识符、设备信息和广告互动数据以投放和衡量广告。BikeCue 不创建账号，也不使用云同步。
