BikeCue Support

BikeCue keeps bicycle checks, maintenance dates, cycles, notes, and activity on your device.

English

Track tire pressure, brakes, chain care, lights and visibility, quick releases and axles, helmets and gear, bike shop follow-ups, and a pre-ride ABC Quick Check. Set your own dates, reminder lead times, and notes. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

Apple Reminders is optional. A saved task and local notifications remain available when Apple Reminders permission is not granted.

BikeCue is a reminder and record tool. It does not guarantee safe riding or replace manufacturer instructions or a qualified bicycle mechanic. For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
