BorrowCue Privacy

English

BorrowCue stores loan names, categories, copy counts, due dates, reminder lead times, return branches, card notes, pickup notes, and custom notes on your device.

This release does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Notification permission is optional and is used only for local loan due reminders.

当前版本不需要账号，不包含广告，不使用分析或第三方追踪 SDK。

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地借阅到期提醒。
