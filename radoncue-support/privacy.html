RadonCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

English

RadonCue stores task names, categories, locations, dates, cycles, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets.

Notification permission is optional and is used for local radon test reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated tasks.

RadonCue does not collect data, create accounts, use cloud sync, or include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may show a recommendation for another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap it.

简体中文

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地氡检测提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，只在你选择同步带日期任务后使用。

RadonCue 不收集数据、不创建账号、不使用云同步，也不包含第三方广告或追踪 SDK。它可能推荐同一开发者的另一款 App；只有你主动点击后才会打开 App Store。
