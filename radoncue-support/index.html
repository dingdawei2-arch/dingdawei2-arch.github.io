English

Track short-term radon tests, long-term retests, result notes, mitigation follow-up, post-mitigation retests, home-change retests, annual reviews, and professional service follow-up. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

Apple Reminders is optional. A saved task and local notifications remain available when Apple Reminders permission is not granted.

RadonCue is a reminder and record tool. It does not measure radon, interpret results, diagnose health risk, guarantee safety, compliance, or mitigation performance, or replace official test-kit instructions, laboratory reports, qualified professionals, or local requirements. For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
