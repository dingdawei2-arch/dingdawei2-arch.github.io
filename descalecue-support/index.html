DescaleCue Support

English

DescaleCue is a private local reminder for coffee makers, kettles, humidifiers, water dispensers, and milk frothers that need descaling or cleaning.

Add an appliance, record the last clean date, cycle, reminder lead, water hardness, supply notes, and location, then keep the next task visible with local reminders and widgets.

DescaleCue does not connect to appliance accounts, diagnose hardware, shop for supplies, use cloud sync, show ads, run analytics, or include tracking SDKs.
