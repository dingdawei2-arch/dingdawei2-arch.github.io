DescaleCue Privacy

English

DescaleCue stores appliance names, categories, counts, cleaning dates, reminder lead times, water-hardness notes, supply notes, locations, and notes on your device.

This release does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Notification permission is optional and is used only for local descaling and cleaning reminders.
