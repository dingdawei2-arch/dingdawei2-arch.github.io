Privacy Policy

English

PowerCue is a private local reminder for backup power, including power banks, rechargeable cells, camera batteries, and travel packs.

Add a power item, record capacity notes, last charged date, location, charge cycle, and optional cable or travel notes, then use local reminders and widgets to keep the next check visible.

PowerCue does not read live battery levels, scan Bluetooth, diagnose hardware, control chargers, sync to cloud services, or promise airline compliance.
