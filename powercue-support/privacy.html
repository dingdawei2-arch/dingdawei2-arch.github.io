PowerCue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-14

English

PowerCue stores power item names, categories, capacity notes, units, last charged dates, charge cycles, reminder lead times, storage locations, travel labels, cable notes, and custom notes on your device.

PowerCue does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics or third-party tracking SDKs in this release.

Notification permission is optional and is used only for local charge-check reminders.

本版本不需要账号、不包含广告，也不使用分析或第三方追踪 SDK。

通知权限是可选的，仅用于本地充电检查提醒。
