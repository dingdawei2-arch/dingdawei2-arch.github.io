SubGuard Support

SubGuard is a private local reminder for subscription life-cycle tracking: trial windows, renewal dates, cancellation notes, missed checks, completed checks, and recurring costs.

English

SubGuard records subscription items, trial start/end dates, next renewal dates, billing cycles, cancellation paths, confirmation notes, missed counts, completed counts, and recent activity.

When you save an item, SubGuard can proactively ask for local notification permission. Local notifications are scheduled on your device. Apple Reminders is optional and used only after you turn it on.

Recurring subscription checks continue to the next billing cycle whether you mark the check complete or leave it unfinished. Unfinished cycles are counted as missed and remain in activity history.

SubGuard shows no ads and does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may recommend another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap. Optional SubGuard Plus unlocks unlimited subscription records. SubGuard does not cancel subscriptions, create Calendar events, use Clock alarms, operate system alarms, import account data, or sign in to providers.

For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
