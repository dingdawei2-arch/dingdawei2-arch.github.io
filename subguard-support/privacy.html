SubGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-23

English

SubGuard stores subscription life-cycle records on your device and in the App Group container used by widgets, including subscription names, trial dates, renewal dates, billing cycles, cancellation paths, reminder lead times, notes, completed counts, missed counts, and recent activity.

Notification permission is optional and used only for local subscription, trial, renewal, and cancellation check reminders. Apple Reminders access is optional and used only after you choose to sync dated records to Apple Reminders.

Recurring checks continue to the next billing cycle whether they are completed or missed. Missed counts and activity history stay on device. SubGuard does not create Calendar events, use Clock alarms, operate system alarms, sign in to providers, import account data, or cancel subscriptions for you.

SubGuard does not collect data, create accounts, use cloud sync, or include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may show a recommendation for another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap it. Optional SubGuard Plus purchase status and subscription records remain on device and are handled through Apple.

简体中文

SubGuard 会将订阅生命周期记录保存在你的设备和小组件使用的 App Group 容器中，包括订阅名称、试用日期、续费日期、计费周期、取消入口、提前提醒、备注、已完成次数、未完成次数和最近活动。

通知权限是可选的，只用于本地订阅、试用、续费和取消检查提醒。Apple 提醒事项权限也是可选的，仅在你选择将带日期记录同步到 Apple 提醒事项后使用。

周期检查无论完成还是漏做，都会继续推进到下一计费周期。未完成次数和活动记录保存在本机。SubGuard 不创建日历事件，不使用时钟闹钟或系统闹钟，不登录服务商，不导入账号数据，也不代你取消订阅。

SubGuard 不收集数据、不创建账号、不使用云同步，也不包含第三方广告或追踪 SDK。它可能推荐同一开发者的另一款 App；只有你主动点击后才会打开 App Store。可选的 SubGuard Plus 购买状态与订阅记录保留在设备上，并通过 Apple 处理。
