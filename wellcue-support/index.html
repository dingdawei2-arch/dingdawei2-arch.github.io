English

Track private-well water test reminders, well cap checks, sediment filters, pressure tank checks, pump-area observations, result notes, and professional service follow-up. Local notifications and widgets show the next task.

Apple Reminders is optional. A saved task and local notifications remain available when Apple Reminders permission is not granted.

WellCue is a reminder and record tool. It does not test water quality, diagnose contamination, guarantee drinking-water safety, pressure, compliance, or equipment performance, or replace laboratory testing, product instructions, qualified professionals, or local requirements. For support, email dingdawei2@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and the issue.
