TagCue Support

English

TagCue is a private local reminder for item tracker battery changes, attachment checks, travel tags, and spare trackers.

Add a tracker, record the attached item, last battery or check date, cycle, reminder lead, location, and notes, then keep the next check visible with local reminders and widgets. TagCue includes no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may recommend another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap.

TagCue does not connect to Find My, Tile, Chipolo, Bluetooth, maps, or cloud sync. Read the privacy page.
