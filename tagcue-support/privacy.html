TagCue Privacy

English

TagCue stores tracker names, categories, attached items, counts, battery or check dates, reminder lead times, locations, and notes on your device.

TagCue does not collect data or include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. It may recommend another app from the same developer; the App Store opens only after you tap.

Notification permission is optional and is used only for local tracker check reminders.
